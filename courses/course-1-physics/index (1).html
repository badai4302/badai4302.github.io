Course · PHYS-1410 / PHYS-2110

Physics I

A detailed study of one- and two-dimensional motion: kinematics, Newton's Laws, energy, momentum, and rotational motion, with oscillations, fluids, and thermal physics as time allows. Taught in two tracks — PHYS-1410 (Algebra & Trigonometry) and PHYS-2110 (Calculus) — meeting together for lecture and separately for recitation.
