About Me

I'm originally from Sri Lanka, where I completed my bachelor's degree before moving to the United States for graduate school. I earned my Ph.D. in Physics from the University of North Dakota, then taught for several years in Minnesota before joining the faculty here in Scottsbluff, Nebraska — this is my tenth year teaching here.

Outside the classroom, I love to travel and take every chance I get to explore somewhere new.
